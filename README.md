# Wedding Website – Renan & Heloisa

Welcome to our virtual space!  
This is our wedding website, created to share all the details of this special moment. Here you will find information about the venue, date, time, gift list, and much more.

## About us

Our story began 1 year ago. Since then, we’ve built a relationship based on love, companionship, and joy. Now we’ve decided to make it official and celebrate this moment with the people who matter most to us.

![Our wedding animation](public/gif/gifrenanheloisa.gif)

## Development and features

This project is constantly evolving.  
Over the past few months, I updated what was once a simple HTML, JS, and CSS project, transforming it with React, NextJS, and Shadcn, along with a redesigned layout (still with some details left to refine).  
Currently, I’m working on the **integration with n8n** and **Postgres** to automate guest management and make the experience more interactive.

## Credits

This website was developed by Renan and Heloisa for personal use in their wedding.  
If you reuse this model, please credit us by mentioning our names and/or linking to this repository. Thank you for your understanding!
